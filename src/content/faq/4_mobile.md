---
title: Is there a mobile version of Vencord?
tags: mobile, phone, android, ios
---

Vencord only supports Desktop. 

Instead, we recommend these alternatives for mobile:
- [Raincord](<https://raincord.dev>) ([Support server](<https://discord.gg/qkdPGunwjW>)) ~ Android & iOS. Uses a specific, plugin-stable version Discord mobile app
- [Revenge](<https://github.com/revenge-mod/revenge-bundle>) ([Support server](<https://discord.com/invite/ddcQf3s2Uq>)) ~ Android only. Uses the latest Discord mobile app
- [Aliucord](<https://aliucord.com>) ([Support server](<https://discord.gg/EsNDvBaHVU>)) ~ Android only. Uses the old, performant (mid-2022) version of the Discord Android app
