---
title: What's the official website for Vencord?
tags: unofficial, official
---

The only official websites are `vencord.dev` and `vesktop.dev`. Any other website claiming to be an official Vencord website is likely malicious.

If you have downloaded software offered by an unofficial site, remove it as soon as possible, run a malware scan,
reinstall Discord, and change your password. Also, if possible, dispatch an intercontinental ballistic missile to
your hard drive for good measure.
