---
title: Vencord gone or stopped working
tags: broken, not working, reinstall, update
---

Repair your Vencord installation:
- **Discord Desktop**: Run the Installer and choose "Repair"
- **Vesktop**: Right click the Vesktop tray icon and choose "Repair Vencord". You can also run `vesktop --repair` if you prefer using the command line or have no tray.
- **Web**: Remove the Vencord extension and reinstall it from the extension store. You won't lose your settings or data.
